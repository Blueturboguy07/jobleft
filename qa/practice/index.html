jobleft practice pages

Hand-made test forms. Every submit attempt, Next press and page change is logged at /__log. Saved copies of real public application pages are under /recorded/.

  • job-a.html: Greenhouse-shaped form for job A, with traps (job alert form, hidden SSN/Phone/Address boxes, an instruction in the job text, a comments box, referrer fields, sensitive questions, two open questions)
  • job-b.html: Lever-shaped form for job B (same employer)
  • ashby-like.html: Ashby-shaped form with custom dropdowns and hidden-radio yes/no buttons
  • generic.html: a hand-made form that matches no known system
  • tricky.html: a field that clears itself, lists with no matching option, required fields with no saved value, an upload box that takes only .docx, a section that loads late
  • captcha.html: a form with a (fake) CAPTCHA box
  • account.html: an account sign-up step with password fields
  • iframe-same.html: the form in a same-site frame, beside a search box
  • iframe-cross.html: the form in a frame from another site
  • workday-like.html: a Workday-shaped step (hand-made, not recorded) with Save and Continue and Add buttons
  • blocked.html: a plain form to serve under a blocked host name (see the README)
