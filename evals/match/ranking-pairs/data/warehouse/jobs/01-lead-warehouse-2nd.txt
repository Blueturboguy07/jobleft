Title: Lead Warehouse Associate - 2nd Shift
Company: Contoso Distribution
Location: Round Rock, TX

We are looking for a Lead Warehouse Associate to guide our second shift team. You will assign daily picking and replenishment work, train associates on our RF guns and warehouse management system, run cycle counts, and make sure trucks are loaded on time. You should have 2+ years of warehouse experience, some time leading others, and a forklift certification (we can recertify you). Must be able to lift 50 lbs.

Why work with us? We offer medical, dental and vision insurance, a 401(k) with company match, paid time off and an employee assistance program.
